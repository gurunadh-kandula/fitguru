    Auto-Logging & Apple Watch
    How your workouts save & sync on iPhone
    • 1. Instant Auto-Log on Tap: Tapping any square ✓ button immediately turns it green and writes a dated entry into your Workout & 5 km Walk History in the Progress tab.
    • 2. Browse & Switch Plans: Open Find (or tap 🗂 Plans) to inspect full multi-day splits like 5 Day Muscle Mass Split or switch back to your Home 5kg + 5km Walk Plan anytime.
    • 3. Apple Watch Strength Sync (Mon / Wed / Fri): Start Traditional Strength Training in your Apple Watch Workout app to record active calories & heart rate to Apple Health.
    • 4. Apple Watch 5 km Walk Sync (Tue / Thu / Sat): Start Outdoor Walk on Apple Watch with a 5.0 km target while logging your completed walk in FitGuru.
